Setup Guide

How to Set Up IPTV on Your Smart TV in Under 10 Minutes

Whether you own a Samsung, LG, Android TV, or Amazon Fire Stick, getting IPTV running is simpler than you think. This guide walks you through every step.

What You Need Before Starting

  • A Danke TV subscription (any plan works)
  • A stable internet connection (minimum 10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K)
  • Your Smart TV, Fire Stick, Android TV box, or mobile device
  • Your IPTV credentials (username, password, server URL) — sent to your email after signup

Step 1: Choose Your IPTV Player App

The first step is installing an IPTV player. Here are the best options for each platform:

  • Samsung / LG Smart TV: IPTV Smarters Pro, Smart IPTV (siptv.eu)
  • Android TV / Google TV: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro
  • Amazon Fire Stick: TiviMate (sideload), IPTV Smarters Pro
  • iOS / iPhone / iPad: GSE Smart IPTV, IPTV Smarters
  • Android Phone / Tablet: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro

Step 2: Install the App

Open your device's app store (Google Play, Amazon Appstore, or Apple App Store) and search for the player app. Download and install it. For Samsung and LG, search directly in the built-in app store.

Step 3: Enter Your Danke TV Credentials

Open the IPTV player app and select "Add Playlist" or "Login with Xtream Codes API." Enter the details from your welcome email:

  • Server URL: Your Danke TV server address
  • Username: Your assigned username
  • Password: Your assigned password

Step 4: Start Watching

Once your credentials are accepted, the app loads your channel list. Browse by category — Live TV, Sports, Movies, Series — and press play. Most setups take under 10 minutes from start to first stream.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Buffering? Check your internet speed. Switch to a wired Ethernet connection for best results.
  • Channels not loading? Double-check your credentials. Contact our 24/7 support if the issue persists.
  • ISP blocking? Enable the Shield+ Proxy add-on in your Danke TV plan to bypass restrictions.
